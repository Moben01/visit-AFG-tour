Practical Information

Everything you need to know for your journey

Visa & Entry

Most visitors require a visa. Apply at Afghan embassies before travel. Ensure passport validity of at least 6 months.

  • Visa required for most nationalities
  • Apply in advance
  • Multiple entry options available

Transportation

Domestic flights connect major cities. Road travel varies in quality. Taxis and private drivers are common in cities.

  • Domestic flights available
  • Taxis in urban areas
  • Variable road conditions

Accommodation

Range from basic guesthouses to international hotels in major cities. Book in advance, especially during peak seasons.

  • Hotels in major cities
  • Guesthouses available
  • Advance booking recommended

Health & Safety

Comprehensive travel insurance essential. Medical facilities vary. Drink bottled water and take food precautions.

  • Vaccinations recommended
  • Hygiene important
  • Check travel advisories

Communication

Mobile networks cover urban areas. Internet access available in cities. SIM cards can be purchased locally.

  • Local SIM cards available
  • Internet in urban areas
  • Variable network coverage

Culture & Etiquette

Conservative Islamic society. Respect local customs. Dress modestly. Ask permission before photography.

  • Modest dress expected
  • Photography restrictions
  • Religious respect important

Regional Guide

Discover Afghanistan's diverse regions

Kabul Region

The capital city offers historical sites, museums, and bustling markets. Modern amenities alongside traditional culture.

Highlights:
  • National Museum of Afghanistan
  • Paghman Gardens
  • Chicken Street markets

Northern Region

Mazar-i-Sharif and Balkh province feature ancient historical sites and the famous Blue Mosque.

Highlights:
  • Blue Mosque (Rawze-e-Sharif)
  • Ancient Balkh ruins
  • Agricultural heartland

Western Region

Herat showcases Persian influences with magnificent architecture and rich cultural heritage.

Highlights:
  • Herat Citadel
  • Friday Mosque
  • Traditional handicrafts

Central Highlands

Bamyan province offers stunning natural beauty and the historical site of the Buddha niches.

Highlights:
  • Buddha niches
  • Band-e-Amir lakes
  • Trekking opportunities
