What is an Afghanistan Heritage Pass?

An Afghanistan Heritage Pass is a proposed tourism initiative that would allow visitors to explore Afghanistan's most iconic cultural and historical sites while saving money.

Instead of paying separately at each location, travelers could purchase a pass granting access to multiple attractions — with potential savings of up to 60%.

Possible Included Attractions:
  • National Museum of Afghanistan (Kabul)
  • Babur's Gardens
  • Darul Aman Palace
  • Herat Citadel
  • Bamiyan Valley and Buddhas
  • Blue Mosque – Mazar-i-Sharif
  • Band-e-Amir National Park
Two Types of Passes Could Be Offered:
Fixed Attractions Pass

Choose 3 to 7 attractions and visit them anytime within your trip duration. Ideal for short stays or focused visits.

Unlimited Cultural Pass

Visit as many sites as you like within 3, 5, or 7 consecutive days. Perfect for regional or cultural explorers.

While this system is not officially available in Afghanistan yet, such an initiative could help simplify tourism, boost cultural preservation, and provide better access to travelers. For now, visitors can arrange bundled site visits through tour operators or cultural NGOs.

Note: This is a conceptual guide; no official Afghanistan Heritage Pass currently exists.

How to Use an Afghanistan Heritage Pass

Using your Afghanistan Heritage Pass would be as simple as opening a mobile app.

The process would be similar for any future pass provider, with passes accessible via mobile apps on your smartphone.

  1. Decide which pass suits your trip — consider how long you'll stay and how many cultural sites you want to visit.
  2. Create an account on the pass provider's website, and purchase your pass online.
  3. Download the provider's mobile app from the Google Play Store or Apple App Store.
  4. Sign in to the app, and your pass will be ready to use.
Important Notes

For most attractions, you would show a barcode or QR code on your app at entry. Some sites may require advance booking — so check details before your visits to ensure a smooth experience.

Always verify current security conditions and travel advisories before visiting any sites in Afghanistan.

If you're planning to visit several tourist sites across Afghanistan, getting a tourist pass can save you money and time.
