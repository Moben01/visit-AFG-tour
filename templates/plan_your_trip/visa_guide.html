Afghanistan Visa Types

Explore the various visa options available for visiting Afghanistan

Tourist Visa

Perfect for travelers exploring Afghanistan's rich cultural heritage and natural beauty.

  • Single or multiple entry
  • 30-90 days validity
  • Extendable

Business Visa

Ideal for professionals attending meetings, conferences, or exploring business opportunities.

  • Multiple entry options
  • 6-12 months validity
  • Letter of invitation required

Transit Visa

For travelers passing through Afghanistan to another destination.

  • Short-term stay
  • Up to 7 days validity
  • Onward ticket required

Student Visa

For international students pursuing education in Afghan institutions.

  • Long-term stay
  • Acceptance letter required
  • Renewable annually

Work Visa

For professionals employed by Afghan companies or organizations.

  • Long-term stay
  • Acceptance letter required
  • Renewable annually

Residence Visa

For those planning to establish long-term residence in Afghanistan.

  • Long-term validity
  • Financial proof required
  • Family sponsorship options

Find answers to common questions about Afghanistan visas

Standard processing time is 5-10 business days for most visa types. Expedited processing may be available for an additional fee, which can reduce processing time to 3-5 business days. During peak travel seasons, processing times may be longer.

Yes, most tourist visas can be extended for an additional 30-60 days by applying at the Department of Immigration in Kabul. You must apply before your current visa expires and provide a valid reason for the extension. Additional fees apply for visa extensions.

Typically, you will need:
  • Completed application form
  • Passport with at least 6 months validity
  • Two recent passport-sized photographs
  • Proof of travel itinerary
  • Hotel reservations or invitation letter
  • Proof of sufficient funds for your stay
  • Visa application fee payment

While not always mandatory for visa issuance, comprehensive travel insurance is highly recommended for all visitors to Afghanistan. Your insurance should cover medical emergencies, evacuation, trip cancellation, and personal liability. Some visa categories may require specific insurance coverage.
